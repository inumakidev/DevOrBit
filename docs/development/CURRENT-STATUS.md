### Strict trust-policy parser implementation and validation — 2026-10-21

The internal parser implementation, tests, and fixed digest vectors are present in:

- `packages/registry/src/provenance/trust-policy.ts`
- `tests/unit/registry/provenance/trust-policy.test.ts`
- `tests/fixtures/registry/trust-policy-vectors.json`

The parser accepts raw `Uint8Array` input; applies the approved 1 MiB raw-input and policy-specific JCS/collection budgets; rejects shared-memory input, BOM, malformed UTF-8/JSON, duplicate decoded keys, and missing/unknown schema fields; validates exact sequence values, canonical repository/key identifiers and key fingerprints, timestamps and validity intervals, ordered unique arrays, and signer scopes; and hashes RFC 8785 JCS policy bytes. It is internal and remains outside public package exports. It does not parse or verify signed updates, authenticate bootstrap, verify trust chains, authorize signers, enforce revocations, inspect protected high-water state, or integrate registry discovery.
